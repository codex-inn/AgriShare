AgriShare terms of use

Prototype terms · last updated 13 October 2026. These preliminary terms are informational and require institutional/legal review before commercial launch.

What AgriShare provides

AgriShare is a student prototype for discovering owner-published agricultural equipment listings and coordinating booking or group-work requests. Listings, prices and availability are supplied by users and may change. The app does not guarantee that a machine, operator, price, or date is genuine or available.

Farmers and equipment owners

  • Publish only accurate information you are authorized to share.
  • Confirm machine condition, operator qualifications, service area, dates, charges, transport and safety requirements directly before work begins.
  • Obtain consent before sharing another person's contact information or photographs.
  • Do not impersonate others, post unlawful content, harass users or misuse booking details.
  • Report suspected fraud or unsafe listings to the project maintainers.

Payments, reviews, disputes and verification

Payments and wallets are not implemented. Do not assume that an in-app booking is a payment, contract, insurance or guarantee. Reviews may be submitted only by participants in a completed booking and are visible only to those participants; they do not independently establish identity, equipment condition or service quality. The prototype does not provide a formal dispute-resolution or insurance service.

Weather and crop analysis

Weather forecasts may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific field. CropSight results, if the model service is configured, are automated estimates and are not a diagnosis or pesticide recommendation. Verify important decisions with local conditions and qualified agricultural extension personnel.

Support requests

Signed-in users may submit private support, listing-safety, account-access or account-deletion requests. Project maintainers review these manually; there is no guaranteed response time, and an account-deletion request does not delete the account automatically. Do not submit passwords, identity documents or payment credentials.

Availability and responsibility

The service may be unavailable, incomplete or changed during development. Use it at your own discretion and do not submit sensitive data. These preliminary terms do not limit rights that cannot lawfully be limited. Before public commercial use, the project team must publish reviewed terms, contact details, provider rules, safety guidance and a complaint process.
