Simple by design

From search to field in three steps

A straightforward flow for farmers, machinery owners and village-level coordinators.

01

Discover nearby equipment

Search by machine type, location and rate. Check availability and contact the owner to confirm the real schedule.

02

Book solo or join a group

Send a booking request for your field, or join neighbours to reach a shared acreage target and reduce mobilization costs.

03

Coordinate and confirm

Agree on timing, final price and service scope with the owner. Complete the work and share feedback after service.

For machinery owners

Put your equipment to work.

List machinery you own, share your service area and set a clear rate. AgriShare can help you reach more farmers and organize requests in your area. Verify every booking directly until live platform services are configured.

Project & community

A practical digital tool for agriculture

AgriShare is designed as a student-led prototype for local agricultural machinery access, cooperative booking and more efficient field operations.

🌱

Institutional context

Tamil Nadu Agricultural University
Agricultural College & Research Institute, Vazhavachanur, Tiruvannamalai.

🤝

Community impact

Improve discovery of underused machinery, make group coordination easier and encourage transparent rates for small and marginal farmers.

🛠️

Prototype status

Interactive demo with browser-local sample data. Live authentication, shared database, verified providers, payments and real-time availability require setup.

Request a booking

Share your requirements with the equipment owner.

Your request is shared with the equipment owner through AgriShare. Include contact details only if you consent to share them with that owner. A request is not a confirmed booking.

Create a group booking

Bring nearby fields together for one coordinated machine visit.

Your signed-in account is recorded as the organizer. Do not add private contact details here; confirm final rates and availability before arranging the machine.

List your equipment

Publish your equipment for nearby farmers to discover.

Your listing will be visible to visitors. Do not include personal phone numbers or sensitive information in the description. Listings are not identity-verified unless explicitly marked by the platform.

Use your email to create an account or sign in.
