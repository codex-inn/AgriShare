AgriShare privacy notice

Prototype notice · last updated 12 October 2026. This notice describes the intended use of data in this student prototype. It is not a substitute for an institution-approved privacy policy or legal review.

Information the app may process

  • Username and authentication identifiers needed to sign in.
  • Equipment listing details that an owner chooses to publish, such as equipment name, category, rate, village/service area and optional photo URL.
  • Booking details supplied by a farmer, including preferred date, acreage, field location, notes and phone number where the booking form requests it.
  • Group booking and membership details needed to coordinate shared work.
  • Messages exchanged by the farmer and equipment owner for an active booking.
  • A village or town entered for weather or map lookup. The weather/map lookup sends that search text to the relevant public provider; the app does not need precise device location for this feature.
  • A crop image only when the user submits it for analysis; it is sent to the configured CropSight API.

How data is used

Account data supports sign-in. Public equipment listings and open group summaries support discovery. Booking details and private messages support coordination between the involved farmer and equipment owner. Weather lookup uses Open-Meteo; map tiles use OpenStreetMap. Crop images are sent to the CropSight endpoint configured by the user. Review each provider's own privacy terms before submitting information.

Visibility and safety

Do not publish passwords, identity documents or sensitive personal information. A phone number supplied for a booking should be shared only for the stated coordination purpose and with the user's consent. Public listing visibility depends on the live database policies; do not assume all fields are private without verifying the deployed schema and policies. No payment processing is provided by this prototype.

Retention and account requests

Data may remain in the connected Supabase project until removed through an available account/data management process or by an authorized project administrator. A self-service account deletion flow is not currently guaranteed. For questions or deletion requests, contact the project maintainers through the repository issue tracker; do not post personal details publicly in an issue.

Security and limitations

The project uses Supabase authentication and row-level security, but the full deployed system still requires review and testing. Do not rely on this prototype for sensitive or urgent transactions. Before public launch, the project team must identify a responsible privacy contact, define retention periods, validate all table policies, and obtain institutional/legal review.
